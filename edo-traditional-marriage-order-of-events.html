Edo traditional marriage: the order of events

The Edo traditional marriage has its own favourite moments, like the decoy brides and the lap-sitting. Here is the order most families follow. Bini, Esan and Afemai families can differ.

Order of events

  1. Arrival and seating of both families
  2. Opening prayer
  3. Kola nut and drinks from the bride's family
  4. The groom's spokesman states the purpose of the visit
  5. The groom's family presents kola, drinks and gifts
  6. The bride's family checks the marriage list
  7. Bride price is paid (most of it is returned)
  8. Decoy brides! The groom must find his wife
  9. The bride enters and is asked if she accepts
  10. The bride sits on her father's lap for prayers
  11. Lap-sitting with the groom's father, then the groom
  12. The couple tastes honey, sugar and bitter kola
  13. Charge and prayers led by the Okaegbe
  14. The groom thanks his in-laws with a drink
  15. The couple dances out. Food, music and dancing
  16. Ovbioha: the bride is escorted to her husband's home

What families usually bring

  • Holy Bible
  • Kola nuts
  • Palm wine
  • Schnapps (2 bottles)
  • Malt (crates)
  • Beer (cartons)
  • Wine (1 carton)
  • Yam tubers
  • Palm oil
  • Bag of salt
  • Honey and sugar
  • Wrapper for the bride's mother
  • Wrapper for the bride's father
  • Bride price (as agreed)
  • Money for the bride's mother
  • Money for the bride's father
  • Money for the men of the family
  • Umbrella / suitcase of wrappers (optional)

Every family's list is different, and some towns add or drop items. Use this as a starting point and check it with your elders.
