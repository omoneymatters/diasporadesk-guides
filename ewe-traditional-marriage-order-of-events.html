Ewe traditional marriage: the order of events

The Ewe marriage ceremony is led by the groom's aunties and a spokesperson, with the knocking drinks, the knocking fee and the marriage items. Here is the order most families follow. Families vary.

Order of events

  1. Arrival of the groom's family, led by his aunties
  2. Opening prayer
  3. Introductions and welcome
  4. The spokesperson states the purpose of the visit
  5. Vofofo (knocking): the knocking drinks are presented
  6. The knocking fee is accepted
  7. Akpedaha: thank-you drink from the groom's family
  8. The aunties present the marriage items
  9. The bride's family checks and accepts the items
  10. The bride is brought out
  11. The bride is asked, and she accepts
  12. Presentation of the ring and Bible
  13. Advice from both sets of parents
  14. Libation and prayers for blessings
  15. Gifts to the in-laws and thanksgiving
  16. The bride is handed over. Refreshments and dancing

What families usually bring

  • Engagement ring
  • Bible
  • Wax print cloth (6 half pieces)
  • Headscarves (6)
  • Sandals, shoes and bags
  • Jewellery sets and watches (2)
  • Underwear and nightwear
  • Waist beads (optional)
  • Schnapps (2 bottles)
  • Gin
  • Soft drinks and malt (crates)
  • Beer
  • For her mother: half piece of cloth and sandals
  • For her father: full piece of cloth and sandals
  • Money for her parents
  • Money for her brothers
  • Knocking fee
  • Thank-you money

Every family's list is different, and some towns add or drop items. Use this as a starting point and check it with your elders.
