Igba Nkwu: the order of events at an Igbo traditional wedding

The Igba Nkwu (wine carrying) is the heart of an Igbo traditional wedding. Here is the usual sequence, from the kola to the bride finding her husband in the crowd. Towns and families do it differently, so treat it as a guide.

Order of events

  1. Arrival and seating of guests
  2. Opening prayer
  3. Presentation and breaking of kola (Iwa Ọjị)
  4. Welcome and introduction of both families
  5. Bride price settled (if not done earlier)
  6. Presentation of the list items to the Umunna
  7. Presentation to the Umuada and the women
  8. Entrance of the bride with her friends
  9. Bride's father hands her the cup of palm wine
  10. The bride searches the crowd for her husband
  11. She kneels, sips and offers him the wine
  12. The couple greets her father and the elders
  13. Prayers and blessings from the elders
  14. Bride's dance: spraying and gifts at her feet
  15. Couple's dance with both families and friends
  16. Vote of thanks, closing prayer and reception

What families usually bring

  • Kegs of palm wine (Mmanya nkwụ)
  • Cartons of beer
  • Crates of soft drinks and malt
  • Bottles of schnapps
  • Kola nuts (Ọjị)
  • Goat
  • Tubers of yam
  • Bag of rice
  • Stockfish
  • George and wrappers for the mother
  • Mother's items (headties, bag, shoes)
  • Father's items (cap, walking stick, attire)
  • Umuada items
  • Umunna items
  • Tobacco or snuff (Ụtaba)
  • Potash (Akanwụ)
  • Hurricane lantern
  • Keg of kerosene
  • Bride price (as agreed)
  • Gifts for the bride

Every family's list is different, and some towns add or drop items. Use this as a starting point and check it with your elders.
