Nigerian funeral programme: order of service and burial rites

Nigerian burial ceremonies often run over several days. Churches and families differ (a Catholic requiem mass is not the same as a Pentecostal service), so check the order with your pastor or priest. This is the common sequence.

The usual sequence

  1. Wake-keep or service of songs (usually the evening before): hymns, prayers, a scripture reading, a few words from family and church, a closing prayer
  2. Funeral service (or requiem mass for Catholic families): processional hymn, prayers, readings, sermon, tributes, offering, committal prayer, benediction, recessional
  3. Interment, often at the family compound or a separate burial ground, with directions given to guests
  4. Reception hosted by the family after the service or burial
  5. Thanksgiving service, often the following Sunday (sometimes called an outing service), which closes the burial ceremonies

What goes in the programme booklet

  • Cover: photo, full name, years, a scripture or short quote
  • Order of service for the funeral service
  • Schedule of burial rites with dates, times and venues
  • Life story or obituary
  • Survived by: spouse, children, grandchildren, siblings
  • Tributes and a favourite scripture or poem
  • Thank-you page and acknowledgments

These are the sections most families include. Add or drop what suits yours.
